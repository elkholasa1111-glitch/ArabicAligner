# Community conduct

ArabicAligner welcomes people contributing code, documentation, examples, bug reports, and usage questions.

- Discuss ideas and behavior constructively; give concrete examples when reporting a problem.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, and backgrounds.
- Keep communication free of harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, and unwanted personal attention.
- Respect privacy and redistribution rights. Do not publish another person's recordings, contact details, or private conversations without permission.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project; avoid spam and promotion unrelated to the discussion.

The [primary maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md) moderates project issues and pull requests and may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should address behavior consistently and proportionately.

For a nonsensitive project conduct concern, open a minimal issue asking for maintainer attention. If the concern involves private or harmful information, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting channel](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) instead of posting that information publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow [Security](SECURITY.md).
